I am a theoretical physicist working at the intersection of high-energy theory and gravitational physics, with particular interest in effective field theory, (extremal) black holes, electric-magnetic duality, and $2d$ models. 

I currently a postdoctoral fellow in Daniel Baumann’s group at [LeCosPA](https://www.lecospa.ntu.edu.tw/) and the Max Planck&ndash;IAS&ndash;NTU Center in Taipei.
I obtained my PhD at Imperial College London with Andrew J. Tolley in 2024. 
See my [CV](https://cyr-chen.com/files/cv.pdf) for more about my background.
